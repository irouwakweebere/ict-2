ABOUT US

The ICT Innovation Center,
University of Nigeria, Nsukka

Our Story

Started in 2006 as a staff ICT training programme, the Center now supports digital skills, e-learning, campus technology, and innovation. In 2016, Zenith Bank donated the ICT Centre, which includes e-learning halls, computer facilities, and a data center.

OUR WORK

What We Do

We support learning and daily campus services through technology.

Digital Skills Training

Practical computer and coding courses for students and staff.

E-Learning & Research Support

Facilities that support digital teaching, learning, and research.

ICT Infrastructure & Services

Website, data center, network, and campus internet services.

Innovation & Collaboration

Working with the university community to develop useful ideas.
